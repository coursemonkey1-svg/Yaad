# Privacy Policy — Yaad

**Last updated: 30 September 2026**

Yaad ("the app") is a personal finance companion that helps you remember
what your money was for. This policy explains what happens to your data
in plain language.

## The short version

**Everything you enter into Yaad stays on your phone.**
We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.
We cannot see, access, or sell your data — because it never leaves your device.

## Data we store (on your device only)

- Transactions you record (amounts, merchants, notes, purposes, receipt images)
- People, lending records and repayments you create
- Your own names (aliases) for merchants
- App settings (currency, language, theme)

This data lives in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone.
It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Data we never collect

- No bank usernames, passwords, PINs, OTPs, or card numbers — the app never
  asks for them and has no feature that could use them.
- No location data.
- No contacts, SMS, or call logs.
- No advertising identifiers, no analytics events, no crash-reporting uploads.

## On-device processing

- Receipt text recognition (OCR) runs **on your phone** using on-device
  machine learning. Receipt images are never uploaded.
- Voice notes use your phone's built-in speech recognition. Audio is
  processed as your device normally handles it; the app does not record
  or upload audio files.
- Spending suggestions are computed from your own history on your phone.

## When data leaves your phone

Only when **you** choose to, through Android's share sheet:

- **Backup / export:** you can create a backup file or CSV and share it
  wherever you like (email, Drive, messaging). That file is yours; what you
  do with it is your choice.
- **Shared receipts:** when you use another app's Share button to send a
  receipt *into* Yaad, that content comes from you, on your phone.

## Permissions the app requests (Android)

- **Receive shared content** — so your bank app or gallery can send receipts
  into Yaad when you tap Share.
- **Read selected images/files** — only files *you* pick (receipt screenshots,
  statement files for import).
- **Microphone** — only when you tap the mic button on the note field, to
  dictate a note. Nothing is recorded in the background.
- **Biometric / device credentials** — only if you enable App Lock in
  Settings, to unlock the app.

## Your control

- **Export:** Settings → Export CSV / Backup — your data in open formats.
- **Delete:** Settings → Delete all my data — permanently erases everything
  on the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes all its data.
- There is no account to delete because there is no account.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose finance tool not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship inside the app
and be posted at the same URL as this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions: open an issue on the app's public repository or
contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
